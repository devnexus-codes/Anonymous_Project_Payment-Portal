Why we are doing this

Some people go to sleep hungry tonight. We can change that.

We are a group of friends who believe help should not wait for a perfect moment. So we made a shirt, and we are using it to fund something real.

Before the year ends, our team will visit an Indigenous community and share food and basic necessities with its members, and reach out to people on the street who have no home. Face to face, with our own hands.

No big event. Just people showing up for people.
